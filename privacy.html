1. Introduction

Early Bird Bakes ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website earlybirdbakes.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site.

We are a small, independent food blog based in Portland, Oregon. We believe in keeping things simple and transparent — both in our recipes and in how we handle your data. By using the Site, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

Information you give us voluntarily

  • Contact form submissions: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Newsletter signups: If you subscribe to our newsletter (coming soon), we collect your email address and optionally your first name.
  • Comments: If you leave a comment on a blog post, we collect your name, email, and comment content. (Comments are currently disabled.)

Information collected automatically

  • Log data: Like most websites, our servers automatically record information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time and date of your visit.
  • Device information: We may collect information about the device you use to access the Site, including operating system and screen size.
  • Analytics data: We use privacy-respecting analytics (see Section 6) to understand how visitors use the Site.

In short: We only collect what we need to run the blog, respond to your messages, and understand what content is helpful. We do not sell your data. Ever.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Purpose Data Used
Responding to your inquiries and messages Name, email, message content
Sending occasional newsletters (if you opt in) Email address, first name
Improving the Site and understanding reader preferences Analytics data (anonymized)
Preventing spam and abuse IP address, submission metadata
Complying with legal obligations As required by law

We will never use your information for purposes other than those listed here without your explicit consent.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies and similar tracking technologies on the Site. Here's what we use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the Site to function properly (e.g., remembering if you've dismissed a banner).
  • Analytics cookies: We use a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not track you across other websites and does not store personal identifiers.
  • Preference cookies: If you interact with certain features (like the search bar), we may store a preference in your browser's local storage.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not participate in cross-site tracking networks.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some Site functionality, but the core content will remain accessible. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use trusted third-party services (like an email delivery platform or analytics provider) that process data on our behalf. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court order or subpoena).
  • Protection of rights: We may disclose information when we believe it is necessary to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our readers or others.

6. Third-Party Services

We use a few carefully selected third-party services to help run the blog. Each has its own privacy policy, and we've chosen them with your privacy in mind:

  • Hosting: Our Site is hosted by a reputable provider that implements industry-standard security measures.
  • Analytics: We use a privacy-respecting analytics service that does not track individual users across the web. All data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Fonts: We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may collect your IP address when fonts are served. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Email: If you contact us, your message is processed by our email provider and stored securely.

We do not use social media tracking pixels, advertising networks, or any other invasive third-party tools.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. We extend these rights to all our readers, regardless of location:

  • Right to access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to deletion: You can ask us to delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to restrict processing: You can ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Right to object: You can object to our processing of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

For EU/UK visitors: We process your data based on legitimate interests (running a blog) and consent (when you voluntarily submit information). You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Using HTTPS encryption across the entire Site.
  • Limiting access to personal data to only those who need it (basically just Maya).
  • Regularly reviewing our data practices and third-party providers.
  • Storing data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Early Bird Bakes is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Parents and guardians: if your child loves baking (wonderful!), please supervise their internet use and consider using parental control tools.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we do, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Post a notice on the homepage if the changes are significant.
  • Keep prior versions archived for your reference (available upon request).

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out:

  • Email: privacy@earlybirdbakes.com
  • Contact form: earlybirdbakes.com/contact
  • Mail: Early Bird Bakes, PO Box 1234, Portland, OR 97201

We're a small operation, but we take privacy seriously. Expect a personal reply within 2–3 days.

Thank you for trusting Early Bird Bakes. We're here to share recipes, not your data. If anything in this policy is unclear, just ask — we're happy to explain.
