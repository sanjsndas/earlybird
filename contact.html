Quick Answers

Do you accept guest posts?
Rarely, but I'm open to pitches from fellow bakers. Send me a short outline and a link to your work. No sponsored content, ever.
Can I republish a recipe?
You're welcome to share a link and quote a short excerpt with credit. Full recipes require written permission. See my Terms page for details.
Do you do custom recipe development?
I occasionally take on freelance recipe development and food writing. Reach out with your project details and timeline.
Why didn't my bread rise?
Nine times out of ten, it's the yeast — either expired or the liquid was too hot. Check out my full troubleshooting guide on the blog for more help.
