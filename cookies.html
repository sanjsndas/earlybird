1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give site owners information about how their site is being used.

Cookies can be "persistent" (they remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (they are deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by a service we use).

We also use similar technologies like local storage and pixels, which function similarly to cookies. This policy covers all of these technologies, but we'll refer to them collectively as "cookies" for simplicity.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a few simple, practical reasons:

  • To make the Site work properly: Some cookies are essential for basic functionality, like remembering your preferences or ensuring security.
  • To understand how you use the Site: We use privacy-respecting analytics to see which recipes and articles are most helpful. This helps us create better content for you.
  • To remember your choices: For example, if you dismiss a notification or set a preference, a cookie helps us remember that so you don't have to do it again.

In short: We use cookies to keep the Site running smoothly and to learn what content you enjoy. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites, and we do not sell any data collected through cookies.

We keep our cookie usage to an absolute minimum. If we ever add new cookies, we'll update this table and, where required, ask for your consent.

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the Site to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences. You can set your browser to block these cookies, but some parts of the Site may not work as a result.

Analytics Cookies

We use a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not track you across other websites and does not store any personal identifiers. All data is aggregated and anonymized. These cookies help us understand which recipes are popular and how visitors navigate the Site.

Preference Cookies

These allow the Site to remember choices you make (like your search history or dismissed banners) to provide a more personalized experience. They are not essential, but they make the Site nicer to use.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use any advertising cookies or social media tracking pixels. However, some third-party services we use may set their own cookies:

  • Fonts: We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may set a cookie to serve the fonts correctly. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting: Our hosting provider may use essential cookies for security and load balancing. These are strictly necessary and do not track you.
  • Email: If you contact us via a form, our email provider may use cookies to prevent spam. These are not used for tracking.

We have chosen our third-party providers carefully and only work with those who respect user privacy. We do not allow any third party to use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking on our Site.

5. Managing Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or delete certain cookies. The help section of your browser will tell you how.
  • Opt-out tools: For analytics cookies, you can often opt out directly through the provider's website. Since we use a privacy-focused analytics tool, there is typically no tracking to opt out of.
  • Do Not Track: Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. We respect this signal and do not track users who have it enabled.

Please note that if you disable cookies, some features of the Site may not function properly. For example, we may not be able to remember your preferences.

Quick tip: You can usually find cookie settings in your browser's "Preferences" or "Settings" menu under "Privacy" or "Security." Each browser is a little different, so check your browser's help documentation for specific instructions.

6. Your Consent

When you first visit Early Bird Bakes, you may see a small banner informing you about our use of cookies. By continuing to use the Site, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

If you do not agree to our use of cookies, you should adjust your browser settings to block them or stop using the Site. You can also withdraw your consent at any time by deleting cookies from your browser and adjusting your settings.

For more information about your privacy rights, please see our Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we do, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Post a notice on the homepage if the changes are significant.
  • Where required by law, ask for your consent again.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
